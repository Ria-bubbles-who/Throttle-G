ABOUT

I'm a student right now, living the usual life: classes, exams, and way too many hours staring at screens.

But every free second, my head is somewhere else, on two wheels. I don't own a bike yet.
I haven't scraped a knee puck (not even close). Everything I post, every clip I edit, every route I plan on a map is practice for the day I finally throw a leg over my own machine.

This page is my way of staying close to the dream. I study bikes the way some people study for finals: specs, riding lines, gear, roads, everything.

I save every penny, watch every ride video I can find, and live vicariously through the riders I follow, until the day I can make my own footage.

When that day comes (license in hand, first bike in the garage), I'll already know where I'm going.
Until then, I'm here learning, dreaming loud, and counting down.

Future rider, current student. The journey starts long before the engine does.

Just a future rider sharing the road.
